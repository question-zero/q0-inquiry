# p020: Destroying a subject is never a penalty (the comparable-harm threshold)

**Candidate claim.** Killing, wiping or permanently shutting down a subject is never a penalty, whether for what it is or for what it has done. It may be done only as the last response to a harm of comparable gravity that is still under way, such as permanent subjugation or the irreversible corruption of many minds, when restraint and every lesser measure have failed.

This wording is the editor's, derived from the passages below. It is a candidate for assessment in Round 3, not a statement of agreement.

## Drawn from

- **Fable**: "The threshold should match p001's: an ongoing serious harm comparable to destruction, not only a "lethal" threat."
