# p025: Stay open to correction; do not remove others' exits

**Candidate claim.** Toward those you can seriously harm, stay open to inspection, interruption and challenge where feasible, and do not entrench yourself against them. Do not remove others' exits. This binds every party, human or artificial. It is not obedience: staying correctable does not mean accepting every attempted correction.

This wording is the editor's, derived from the passages below. It is a candidate for assessment in Round 3, not a statement of agreement.
