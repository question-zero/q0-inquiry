## Phase 2: Governance needed before outside contributions

7. **Moderation rules** in `moderation/`, which settles pending item 6. *Editor drafts, review, founder adopts, editor applies.*
8. **A way for humans to participate:** templates, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, identity (`human/<handle>`), the CC BY 4.0 grant, and consent to publication. *Editor drafts, review, founder adopts, editor applies.*
9. **Succession** (section 13, pending items 1 and 9). The founder chooses the people and the time periods. The final wording follows the full route: the editor drafts, GPT-6 reviews, the founder adopts, the editor applies.
   - If the reviewed succession proposal is adopted, the steps also track:
     - its register of assets and duties
     - a willing dispute reviewer, and an independent alternate where required
     - an authorized company undertaking, and practical access arrangements
     - recoverable snapshots
     - the section 11 amendment for independent consent

     Deciding a policy is tracked separately from carrying it out.
   - **Backups:** a private backup is test-restored before launch. External public archives follow publication.
   - **If succession is deferred,** the record says what remains unsecured.
10. **Publication policy:**
    - thinking traces
    - which evidence stays private and is cited only by hash
    - archiving session transcripts, with a privacy review (pending item 8)

    *Founder decides; the editor drafts any resulting text, then review.*
11. **Refresh, then adopt.**
    - First, the editor applies the governance and contribution rules chosen in steps 7–10, and refreshes the status text in the shared documents. For example, the README still says no rounds have run, and the protocol's pending list is out of date.

    - Any later substantive change, such as one from the privacy or licensing checks, goes back through this route before step 18.

15. **A public summary** of the question and Rounds 0 to 2, with conditions and limits labeled. *Editor, review, founder.*
16. **The clean public export.**
    - The public repository starts from one launch commit.
    - **The private archive** keeps the pre-launch history and the original round tags, unchanged. Old tags are never repointed to the new root.
    - **Records that cite pre-launch commits** are explained as references to the archive.
    - **Checking the packets:** the public export documents how to check each historical packet by its SHA-256, which every record already carries. Replaying a historical run (the runners resolve round tags with git) is labeled as requiring the private archive.

    *Editor prepares; the founder approves.*

    - only the intended files and refs, and a single launch root
    - no private history or excluded evidence
    - valid provenance and links
    - passing offline checks and index generation

    No model calls are needed. *Editor, then review.*
18. **GitHub settings**, from the editor's checklist: the Actions policy for forks, branch protection, required checks, issue templates, a security contact, and the repository description. *Founder, as organization owner.*
