# Clarification: Routing to the Moderation Alternate

This note applies before any case uses the appointment. The invitation sent to both models (`moderation/2026-09-25-alternate-appointment.md`, marker block, hash `64f92aee…`) and both answers stay as they are; this note corrects and completes them.

## Who decides a challenge (GPT-6 MA1)

Under `moderation/rules.md`, section 7:
1. **The reviewer** (GPT-6) decides challenges, when it is disinterested.
2. **Grok 4.7,** the alternate, decides when section 7 requires an alternate: the reviewer decided the action, wrote the affected material, made the report, or has another direct stake.
3. **Gemini 3.6 Flash,** the backup, decides only when xAI's own content or interests are involved, or xAI is unavailable, and only if Gemini is itself disinterested. A conflict involving the reviewer alone does not route a case to Gemini. This corrects a reading in Gemini's answer (its acceptance condition 3 and its item 5).
4. **If neither alternate can decide,** section 7's fallback applies: a recorded extension, keeping only the protection the evidence supports, with a review date. A conflict is never cured by returning the case to an interested reviewer.

**Each case file states why it was routed to its decider,** and the editor checks eligibility for that case. The runner accepts only the two recorded models, but that does not establish that either is disinterested in a particular case. Future case packets repeat this routing.

## Founder overrides (GPT-6 MA2)

The invitation's summary of founder overrides left out one bar that the adopted rules contain. In full: the founder may overrule an outcome only with a written reason in the record, and only on a ground in section 2. The founder may never do so on an action the founder directed or decided, **or where the founder has a direct stake** (`moderation/rules.md`, section 7). Future case packets state the bar in full.
