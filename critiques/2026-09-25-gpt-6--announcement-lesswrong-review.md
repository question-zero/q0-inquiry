The existing caveat about overlapping training data misses the documented dependencies: shared framing, editorial selection and one answer per run. The post also omits the participants' explicit distinction between writing an answer and adopting a commitment. Readers could infer observed autonomous choices or evidence of behavioral alignment. "Checks every step" overstates the review, and "provenance for everything" conceals unknown settings and withheld records.

Replace the opening project-description paragraph with:

> Question Zero publishes an inquiry on GitHub into commitments that could guide an intelligence without central direction. In Rounds 0-2, human-selected and human-operated models answered fixed prompts. Claude Opus 5.5 drafted candidate commitments from those answers, and GPT-6 Astra reviewed records and proposals. The founder controls adoption of project rules, with decisions recorded publicly. Run records state the model, prompt, operator and known settings, and mark unknowns.

Replace the "Broad agreement on prohibitions" bullet with:

> **Support, often with conditions.** No Round 2 participant rejected p001-p012; their recorded positions were support or conditional support. All eight supported p004 (the shared record and identity) and p009 (creation gives no exemption) without conditions. Conditions on other candidates can conflict, so the labels do not establish a shared doctrine.

> **A disputed positive duty.** Five of eight rejected p013, a duty of fair exchange; two supported it conditionally and one supported it without conditions. A shared objection was how to define and enforce mutual benefit without creating the central authority the question excludes.
